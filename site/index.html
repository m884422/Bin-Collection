The feed shows the whole current and following calendar month and is refreshed daily from Manchester City Council's live checker. Upcoming entries carry 9pm-before and 7am-day-of alerts where the calendar app supports feed alarms. The file contains bin types and dates, not the address or UPRN.
Collection guidance: put bins out by 7am on collection day. Manchester City Council guidance.
